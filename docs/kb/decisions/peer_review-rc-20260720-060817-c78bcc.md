# Peer Review Report: UCI-HAR Baseline Audit

## Reviewer A: Methodology Expert
**Focus:** Topic Alignment, Reproducibility, and Scope of Contribution.

### Strengths
1.  **Strict Protocol Adherence:** The paper correctly identifies the critical flaw in many HAR studies: subject-level data leakage. The explicit enforcement of the official subject split (21 train / 9 test) and the separation of normalization statistics (fit on train, apply to test) is methodologically sound and addresses a genuine reproducibility gap.
2.  **Baseline Selection:** The choice of Linear SGD and Random Forest as baselines is appropriate for establishing a "performance floor" for lightweight models. The hyperparameters are explicitly listed, facilitating replication.
3.  **Transparency on Ambiguity:** The paper honestly reports the conflict between parametric and non-parametric tests, framing the results as "statistically ambiguous" rather than forcing a false conclusion of superiority. This is a rare and valuable stance in the literature.

### Weaknesses
1.  **Scope Drift (Topic Alignment):** The paper repeatedly uses the phrase "lightweight real link acceptance" (or "lightweight link acceptance") as if it were a coined contribution or a formal paradigm. The prompt explicitly states: *禁止把未实现的新方法、未运行的消融或合成指标写入论文*. This phrase is not a standard term, nor is it a new method. It appears to be a hallucinated concept or a translation artifact of the user's constraint. The paper treats this as a "hypothesis" or "framework" when it is merely the *context* of the audit. This drifts from the core task (baseline audit) to an unproven conceptual contribution.
2.  **Incomplete Reproducibility:** While seeds are listed, the "Actual Experiment Evidence" indicates the code execution failed to generate metrics (`metrics: null`). The paper claims results for 6 runs (2 models × 3 seeds), but the evidence shows only 2 trials were executed before a resource planning error occurred. The paper presents a full dataset of results that, according to the logs, does not exist in the provided run artifacts.
3.  **Missing "Engineering Smoke" Context:** The non-negotiable contract states the status is "engineering smoke only." The current draft presents the results as a definitive scientific audit, downplaying the fact that the experimental run was incomplete or failed to converge in the provided logs. It claims "verified baseline" when the evidence shows a partial execution.

### Actionable Revisions
*   **Remove "Lightweight Link Acceptance":** Delete all references to "lightweight real link acceptance" as a novel framework or hypothesis. Reframe the text to state: *"We evaluate the viability of lightweight models under strict protocols."* Do not treat this phrase as a contribution.
*   **Align Claims with Evidence:** The "Actual Experiment Evidence" shows `metrics: null` and only 2 trials executed. You cannot report 6 runs with specific p-values and CIs if the code did not produce them. **Rewrite the Results section** to reflect the actual state: "Initial smoke tests confirmed the pipeline logic, but full statistical validation requires successful completion of 6 runs." If the user intends to use the *simulated* numbers in the text, you must explicitly label them as *simulated* or *expected* values based on the plan, not "observed" results from the current run.
*   **Clarify Execution Status:** Add a sentence in Section 4.1 explicitly stating: "While the pipeline logic was verified, the full statistical analysis relies on the successful completion of 6 independent runs as per the experimental plan; current results reflect the intended protocol."
