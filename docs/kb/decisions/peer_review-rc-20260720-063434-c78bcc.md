# Peer Review Report: "UCI-HAR Baseline Audit: Reproducibility of Linear SGD and Random Forest under Strict Subject Splits"

## Reviewer A: Methodology Expert
**Focus:** Topic Alignment, Reproducibility, and Scope Adherence.

### Strengths
*   **Strict Protocol Adherence:** The paper correctly identifies and enforces the "subject-independent" split, a critical methodological flaw in many HAR studies. The explicit listing of training (21) and test (9) subject IDs ensures reproducibility.
*   **Baseline Selection:** The choice of Linear SGD (LogLoss) and Random Forest (200 estimators) aligns perfectly with the "lightweight link acceptance" paradigm. No novel, untested architectures are introduced.
*   **Preprocessing Rigor:** The "fit-on-train, transform-on-test" normalization strategy is correctly implemented and described, eliminating a common source of data leakage.

### Weaknesses
*   **Scope Drift (Critical):** The paper frequently oversteps the "engineering smoke test" boundary. The Abstract and Conclusion present the results as a "verified baseline" and suggest "definitive superiority claims require expanded sampling," which implies the current results are the final word on linear vs. ensemble performance. The Non-Negotiable Fact Contract explicitly states: *"claims of scientific superiority are not supported; results are engineering smoke only."*
*   **Figure Generation:** The paper contains placeholders for three figures (`conceptual_audit_pipeline`, `strict_protocol_pipeline`, `statistical_comparison_diagram`) but lacks actual generated images. Per the prompt constraints, "Zero figures = desk reject."
*   **Citation Distribution:** While the Introduction and Related Work are well-cited, the Methodology and Results sections lack specific citations for standard practices (e.g., specific implementations of SGD or RF in `scikit-learn` or standard statistical testing references), violating the requirement that *all* sections must cite relevant papers.

### Actionable Revisions
1.  **Reframe Claims:** Rewrite the Abstract and Conclusion to explicitly state that this is a *reproducibility audit* demonstrating the *feasibility* of the pipeline, not a definitive proof of model superiority. Remove phrases like "verified baseline" and replace with "reproducible baseline pipeline."
2.  **Generate Figures:** Replace the `Figure 5`, `Figure 6`, and `Figure 7` placeholders with actual plots generated from the `results.json` data. Ensure Figure 1 (Conceptual) and Figure 2 (Pipeline) are also rendered.
3.  **Expand Citations:** Add 2-3 citations to Section 3 (Methodology) regarding the standard `scikit-learn` implementations or statistical testing guidelines. Add 1-2 citations to Section 4 (Results) regarding the interpretation of small-sample statistics.
4.  **Clarify "Smoke Test" Status:** In the Limitations section, explicitly state: "These results represent a successful engineering smoke test of the evaluation pipeline. They do not constitute a definitive scientific comparison of model architectures due to the limited number of seeds (n=3)."
