## Justification
The decision is **REFINE** because the experiment fails to meet the **MINIMUM QUALITY CRITERIA** required for a **PROCEED** decision, specifically Criterion #3 and Criterion #4.

1.  **Violation of Criterion #3 (Seed Count):** The criteria explicitly state: "Each condition has results from ≥3 seeds."
    *   **Evidence:** The `Results` section states `n_seeds`: `mean=3.000000`, but the metric statistics show `n=2` (e.g., `accuracy_mean`: `n=2`, `f1_macro_mean`: `n=2`). The `NON-NEGOTIABLE RESEARCH READINESS` JSON confirms `total_runs: 2`. This indicates that while the *plan* may have requested 3 seeds, the actual execution only produced 2 valid runs per condition (or the aggregation logic is flawed). Without 3 distinct seeds per condition, statistical robustness is insufficient for paper writing.

2.  **Violation of Criterion #4 (Ablation Integrity):** The criteria state: "No identical per-seed values across different conditions."
    *   **Evidence:** The `Results` section shows that for `accuracy_mean`, the `min` (0.921050) and `max` (0.934510) values are extremely close to the `mean` (0.927780) across only 2 data points. More critically, the `Statistical Analysis` section explicitly states: "No paired statistical test was run because the available artifact does not contain repeated paired samples." This suggests the data structure does not support the required per-seed comparison, risking that the "3 seeds" claimed in the summary are not actually distinct, independent runs that can be compared. If the seeds are not distinct, the ablation integrity is compromised.

3.  **Violation of Criterion #5 (Analysis Quality):** The criteria require an analysis quality rating ≥4/10.
    *   **Evidence:** The `NON-NEGOTIABLE RESEARCH READINESS` block assigns a `plan_quality` score of **45** (likely out of 100, which translates to 4.5/10, but the `readiness_score` is 74.0). However, the `Lessons from Prior Runs` section shows a history of **5 consecutive REFINE** decisions due to "insufficient hypotheses/experiments." The current experiment is labeled a "smoke test" that "failed" the `experiment_run` stage in prior iterations. The analysis explicitly warns: "It must not be presented as scientific evidence unless the provenance explicitly permits scientific claims." Proceeding to writing with a "smoke test" that failed prior rigor checks violates the "Think well" and "Do well" principles of the research skill set.

While the primary metric is defined (Criterion #2) and there are baselines (Criterion #1), the failure to execute the required number of seeds and the history of failed execution make the results insufficient for a paper. The hypothesis mechanism may be sound, but the *experiment design* (execution of seeds) needs re-tuning.

*   **Seed Count Discrepancy:** `Results` section shows `n=2` for metrics, while `n_seeds` summary claims 3. Criterion #3 requires ≥3 seeds.
*   **Execution Failure History:** `Lessons from Prior Runs` lists 5 prior `REFINE` decisions and 1 `Stage experiment_run failed`.
*   **Statistical Limitation:** `Statistical Analysis` explicitly states: "No paired statistical test was run because the available artifact does not contain repeated paired samples."
*   **Readiness Warning:** `NON-NEGOTIABLE RESEARCH READINESS` states `scientific_claims_allowed: false` and `writing_policy: limited_claims_only`.
*   **Metric Variance:** With only 2 data points (`n=2`), the `min` and `max` are insufficient to establish the variance required for a robust scientific claim.

## Next Actions
1.  **Re-run Experiments:** Execute the experiment plan again, ensuring **3 distinct, independent seeds** are run for *each* condition (baseline and proposed method). Verify that the `runs` directory contains 3 separate output files per condition.
2.  **Verify Data Integrity:** Check the `results.json` parsing logic to ensure it correctly aggregates 3 seeds and does not default to 2 due to a configuration error or timeout.
3.  **Validate Baselines:** Ensure the "lightweight real benchmark" is sufficient for the specific hypothesis. If the dataset is too small to show variance between seeds, consider expanding the dataset scope or selecting a more robust benchmark (per `arc-how-to-do-research` "Look well" and "Do well" stages).
4.  **Re-evaluate Hypothesis:** Once 3 seeds are obtained, re-run the statistical analysis to check for significant differences. If the variance is still negligible across seeds, revisit the hypothesis mechanism (pivot) to ensure the proposed method actually impacts the metric.
5.  **Update Artifacts:** Update `experiment_final/results.json` and `refinement_log.json` with the new run data before attempting a PROCEED decision.
